How this works

Fastest is the shortest walk on the mapped paths. Driest counts each metre in the open as several metres under cover (you set how many), so it will take a longer way if that keeps you dry.

Under cover means under one of LTA’s covered linkway roofs, or on a path OpenStreetMap marks as covered, indoors or underground. Shelters only count if they are in one of the two maps, so real walks may be drier.
