Why SVY21 is the right tool here

SVY21 is Singapore's own transverse Mercator. Its centre line, 103°50′ E, runs down the middle of the island, and along it the scale is exactly 1. The further you go from that line the more it stretches, but Singapore is too narrow for that to matter: never more than per km on the main island.

UTM 48N is centred on 105° E, east of the island, and shrinks its whole zone by 0.9996 on purpose so errors average out across 6° of longitude. Singapore sits on the shrunk side, so UTM comes up short everywhere.
