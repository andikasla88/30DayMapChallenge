    How to read it. WBGT (wet-bulb globe temperature) folds air temperature, humidity, sunshine and wind into one number for heat stress on the body. Each station reading is a 15-minute average. Between stations the colour is an estimate: each spot is a blend of all the readings, weighted by one over distance squared (inverse-distance weighting), and shown on land only. The hotter a place, the more it shimmers.
