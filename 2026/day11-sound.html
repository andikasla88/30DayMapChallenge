…lightning readings in one day

Each flash sends out a ring of sound at 343 m/s. When it reaches the ear, you hear the thunder. Count the seconds: every 3 s is about 1 km.

  • Cloud-to-ground strike: readings
  • Cloud-to-cloud flash: readings
  • Haze: where the day’s lightning was thickest
  • Its thunder, on the way to you
  • You. Drag the ear, tap the map or use your location

How this was made

Best with headphones. Sound starts only when you press.
