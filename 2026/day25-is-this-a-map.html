Sources and method

The diagram is not LTA’s map. It is generated here from the network alone, by multicriteria hill climbing on a grid after Stott and others (2011), simplified: every stretch runs at a multiple of 45°, stations are spaced as evenly as the network allows, bends are few, lines keep clear of each other and cross only where they really cross, and each stretch leans as little as it can from its true compass direction. The finished diagram is scaled and centred onto the true positions by least squares, without turning it. Between the two ends every station moves in a straight line. Track lines are sgraildata’s simplified, smoothed paths.

Measures: off its true spot is the mean straight-line distance from each station to its real position. Direction kept is the share of the 211 links between neighbouring stations that point within 22.5° of their true bearing. Distance order kept is Spearman’s rank correlation between all 16,836 station-to-station distances on screen and on the ground. All are computed in your browser from SVY21 metres.

Votes are not sent anywhere. Yours stays on this device, and a copied link carries everyone’s marks that it already held, plus yours.
