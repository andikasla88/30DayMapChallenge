Kalimantan, wet again

Borneo's 2026 fire season, redrawn as it could have been: every peat dome rewetted, its drainage canals blocked and its water back near the surface. Wet peat barely burns. Wherever a satellite saw fire on peat this year, there is a ripple instead.

What could be: on 4 Nov 2026 Indonesia's Environment Ministry ordered more than 400 companies on peat to keep their concessions wet, blocking canals and raising water tables (The Jakarta Post). This map imagines all of Borneo's peat wet at once. Peat can still burn in an extreme drought, and the embers on dry land would need other answers.
