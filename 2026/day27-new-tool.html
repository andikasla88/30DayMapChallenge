How deep does Singapore go?

The island as a sheet of glass, with what lies beneath hung at its depth. Bright marks are published depths. Hatched bands are tunnels with no published depth, drawn across LTA’s typical 20–30 m. OpenStreetMap maps – km of underground MRT track (each track counted), with – km more being built.
